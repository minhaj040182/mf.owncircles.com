Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

What is Recirculating Aquaculture System (RAS) technology?

RAS is an indoor, biosecure fish farming method that filters and reuses up to 95-99% of culture water. Water flows sequentially through mechanical filtration (drum filter) to remove solid waste, biological filtration (MBBR biofilter) to convert toxic ammonia into harmless nitrate, oxygen dissolution cones, and UV sterilizers before returning to fish tanks.

How do I calculate Feed Conversion Ratio (FCR) in fish farming?

Feed Conversion Ratio (FCR) is calculated as: FCR = Total Weight of Feed Given (kg) ÷ Net Fish Biomass Gain (kg). A lower FCR indicates higher feed efficiency. Commercial farmers target an FCR between 1.1 and 1.5 depending on species and feed crude protein quality.

What crude protein percentage is required for commercial fish feed?

Fish protein requirements vary by growth stage: Fry and fingerlings require 35% to 40% crude protein, grow-out fish (100g to harvest weight) require 28% to 32% crude protein, and broodstock require 38% to 42% crude protein formulated with essential fatty acids.
